# PAR requirements

Requirement text is taken from spec-addendum-2026-11-06.md.

- PAR-001: List missions and see which one needs you.
- PAR-002: View a mission's tasks, current run, events and capacity.
- PAR-003: Start a mission.
- PAR-004: Pause and resume.
- PAR-005: Stop (including force).
- PAR-006: Add a task.
- PAR-007: Amend a task.
- PAR-008: Change a task's priority or dependencies.
- PAR-009: Steer, every kind.
- PAR-010: Approve with evidence, and reject with a reason.
- PAR-011: Live terminal: open, watch, type; usable at Pixel 4 size.
- PAR-012: View worker health and connection state.
- PAR-013: Sign out and sign in with a password.
- PAR-014: Sign in with a passkey (Chromium virtual authenticator in CI; the real phone is PI-VERIFY-013).
